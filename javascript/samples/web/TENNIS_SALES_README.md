## How it Works

1. **Select a customer type** from the dropdown:
   - **General Customer (browsing)**: Customer who needs guidance choosing any racket
   - **Wilson Shift 99 V1 Customer**: Interested in spin and modern technology
   - **Wilson Blade 100 V9 Customer**: Wants control with forgiveness
   - **Wilson Pro Staff 97 V14 Customer**: Serious player considering advanced control

2. **Choose a voice** for the AI customer
3. **Click "Record"** to start the conversation
4. **The AI will act as a customer** asking questions and expressing concerns
5. **You respond as the salesperson**, providing information and recommendations
6. **After stopping**, click "Analyze Sales Performance" for coaching insights

## Customer Personas (FIXED)

### General Customer (browsing)
- Needs help choosing the right racket
- Asks about different options and features
- Lets you guide them based on their needs

### Wilson Shift 99 V1 Customer
- Intermediate player wanting to upgrade
- Interested in spin generation and control
- Curious about eco-friendly features and modern design
- **Asks questions like**: "Can you tell me about the Shift 99 V1?" "Is it good for spin?"

### Wilson Blade 100 V9 Customer  
- Values precision but wants forgiveness
- Concerned about arm comfort
- Appreciates good looks and technology
- **Asks questions like**: "Is this arm-friendly?" "How does it compare to the Blade 98?"

### Wilson Pro Staff 97 V14 Customer
- Serious player interested in control
- Worried about whether it's too demanding
- Respects the Pro Staff heritage
- **Asks questions like**: "Do I need to be really good to use this?" "What makes the control so precise?"

## Sales Performance Analysis (NEW)

After each conversation, get detailed coaching feedback:

- **Sales Performance Summary**: Overall effectiveness assessment
- **Key Strengths**: What you did well in product knowledge and customer service
- **Areas for Improvement**: Missed opportunities and areas to work on
- **Customer Engagement**: How well you engaged the customer
- **Action Items**: Specific recommendations for improvement
- **Product Knowledge Assessment**: How well you explained features and benefits

## Tips for Success

### As the Salesperson:
- **Listen first**: Let the customer explain their needs
- **Ask qualifying questions**: "What's your current playing level?" "How often do you play?"
- **Translate features to benefits**: Don't just say "16x20 string pattern" - explain "gives you more spin"
- **Address concerns**: If they worry about weight, explain how it affects performance
- **Guide the conversation**: Help them discover what they need

### Customer Behavior to Expect:
- ✅ Asks questions about racket features
- ✅ Expresses concerns about suitability
- ✅ Wants to understand benefits
- ✅ May need reassurance about their choice
- ❌ Won't try to sell you anything
- ❌ Won't provide detailed product specs
