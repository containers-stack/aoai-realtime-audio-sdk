Name: Willson Blade 100 V9

Wilson Blade 100 V9 – Detailed Overview
1. Design & Construction
Aesthetic: The Blade 100 V9 sports a bold Emerald Night matte green finish that immediately stands out on court.

Frame Technology: Features FORTYFIVE° carbon construction, delivering increased flexibility, stability, and that classic Blade "feel".

Handle Integration: Incorporates DirectConnect Technology, which fuses the butt cap directly to the carbon fiber handle—enhancing torsional control and overall stability.

2. Performance Highlights
Power & Forgiveness: Compared to the Blade 98 (16×19), the V9 variant offers slightly more power and greater forgiveness, making it more accessible to a broader range of players while maintaining excellent control.

Beam Comfort: Its 60 RA (softer and more flexible) beam provides a comforting, arm-friendly feel with superb feedback on each shot.

String Pattern & Spin: The 16×19 open string pattern facilitates easy spin generation and depth in shots.

Speed & Maneuverability: With a sub-325 swingweight, the racquet is responsive, quick on the follow-through, and agile at the net or when reacting on defense.

3. Target Player Profile
Skill Level: Ideal for intermediate to advanced players who want precision and control with a forgiving edge.

Feel & Comfort: Delivers the renowned Blade feel—sharp, responsive, and stable—while softening demands on the arm due to its flexible beam and ergonomic design.

Versatility: Balances control, feel, and power, making it a great fit for baseliners who mix aggressive ground strokes with quick net play.

4. User Experience
A hands-on tester summary notes:

“It has just a little more power than the Blade 98... makes it even easier to hit the ball deep... compared to its predecessor, the Blade 100 V8, this version is just a little more stable and even more user-friendly. And yet it continues to perform well under pressure, due to the stability of this racket.”
