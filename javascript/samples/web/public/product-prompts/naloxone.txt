PRODUCT OVERVIEW:
Naloxone is a life-saving medication designed to rapidly reverse opioid overdoses. It works by binding to opioid receptors and displacing opioid molecules, thereby restoring normal respiration in individuals experiencing overdose symptoms. It is considered a critical component of emergency preparedness protocols in both medical and community settings.
