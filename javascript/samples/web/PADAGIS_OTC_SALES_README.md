## How it Works

1. **Select a customer health need** from the dropdown:
   - **General Customer (browsing)**: Customer seeking general OTC medication guidance

   - **Acetaminophen 500mg Customer**: Wants pain/fever relief that's gentle on stomach

   - **Loratadine 10mg Customer**: Looking for non-drowsy allergy relief

2. **Choose a voice** for the AI customer
3. **Click "Record"** to start the consultation

5. **You respond as the sales representative**, providing medication information and safety guidance
6. **After stopping**, click "Analyze Sales Performance" for pharmaceutical coaching insights

## Customer Personas (PHARMACEUTICAL FOCUS)

### General Customer (browsing)

- Asks about OTC medication options and safety
- Wants professional recommendations for their situation

### Ibuprofen 200mg Customer
- Experiences occasional headaches or minor pain
- Interested in effective pain relief

- Needs pain/fever relief that's gentle on stomach
- Worried about liver safety with regular use
- Wants to understand dosing and interactions
- **Asks questions like**: "Is this safer for my stomach?" "What's the maximum daily dose?"

### Diphenhydramine 25mg Customer
- Has trouble falling asleep occasionally
- Concerned about next-day drowsiness and dependency
- Wants occasional-use sleep aid guidance

### Loratadine 10mg Customer
- Suffers from seasonal or environmental allergies
- Needs non-drowsy daily allergy relief
- Wants long-lasting allergy symptom control
- **Asks questions like**: "Will this make me drowsy?" "Can I take this every day during allergy season?"

### Omeprazole 20mg Customer
- Experiences frequent heartburn/acid reflux
- Wants prevention rather than just treatment
- Concerned about long-term use and proper timing
- **Asks questions like**: "How is this different from antacids?" "How long can I safely use this?"

## Tips for Success

### As the Pharmaceutical Sales Representative:
- **Listen first**: Let the customer explain their health concerns and symptoms
- **Ask qualifying questions**: "How long have you been experiencing this?" "Have you tried anything else?"
- **Translate features to benefits**: Don't just say "NSAID" - explain "reduces inflammation and pain"
- **Address safety concerns**: Always discuss potential side effects, interactions, and proper dosing
- **Provide clear guidance**: Explain when to take medication, with or without food, and maximum daily limits
- **Know when to refer**: Recognize when symptoms require medical attention beyond OTC options

### Focus on Customer Safety:
- **Dosing education**: Ensure customers understand proper dosing intervals and maximums
- **Drug interactions**: Ask about other medications and medical conditions
- **Side effect awareness**: Discuss common side effects and what to watch for
- **Proper usage timing**: Explain optimal timing for effectiveness (empty stomach, with food, etc.)
- **Duration guidance**: Clarify how long they can safely use the medication
- **Guide the conversation**: Help them discover what they need

### Customer Behavior to Expect:
- ✅ Asks questions about racket features
- ✅ Expresses concerns about suitability
- ✅ Wants to understand benefits
- ✅ May need reassurance about their choice
- ❌ Won't try to sell you anything
- ❌ Won't provide detailed product specs
